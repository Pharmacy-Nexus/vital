V5 – VISUAL REFINEMENT ONLY
=============================
This version keeps the same core scene and only improves the visual look.

What changed:
1) Phone refinement:
   - more iPhone-like proportions and premium titanium/glass materials
   - cleaner dynamic island + tiny camera detail
   - upgraded camera bump, lens rings, flash, LiDAR feel
   - more accurate side buttons
   - subtle screen reflection for a premium product-shot feel

2) Card refinement:
   - slightly improved proportions and rounded corners
   - cleaner metallic shell / edge highlight
   - glossier premium finish
   - preserved the exact artwork / branding / text / QR / NFC symbol

3) Lighting refinement:
   - slightly cleaner product lighting
   - cooler rim light to help the phone edges read better
   - same mood, no redesign

What did NOT change:
- no redesign of the concept
- no change to the core animation idea
- no change to the brand identity or card artwork
- no change to the overall sequence structure
